Course Schedule

The course meets Thursdays, 4:35–7:35 pm in Lafayette Hall L202. Week 1 (Sep 3) only is a virtual session (link will be emailed); every class after that is in person. There are 16 calendar weeks but only 13 teaching weeks (2 no-class weeks + 1 field trip week). Slides and readings will be linked as the semester progresses.

All five homework assignments (see Syllabus for topics) live in your Posit Cloud workspace as hw1.qmd–hw5.qmd; due dates are noted inline below.

Unit 1 — Foundations

Core skills: R basics, tidyverse, dplyr, data import, EDA, ggplot2 R for Data Science: Intro, Whole Game, Data Transformation, Data Import, EDA, Layers, Communication

Unit 2 — Community Partnership

Week 6 is a normal in-class day (no mid-semester field trip), split between a guest lecture and NBRC’s on-campus project kickoff. Week 7 bridges visualization into project launch.

Week Date Topic Slides Reading

7 Oct 15 Chart types and visual communication; project teams + scope confirmed. Scope & Team Charter due (10% of project grade). Week 7 None
